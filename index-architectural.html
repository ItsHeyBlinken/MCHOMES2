Credentials

Uncommon craft

Mark Clifton is a Certified Graduate Builder (CGB) and Certified Graduate Professional (CGP) through the National Association of Home Builders — bringing decades of experience to every project he touches.

Our individualized approach renders an uncommon result. Each project is unique, and our work is tailored to create a product that is beyond comparison. We specialize in new construction, renovation, and commercial projects.

Mark proudly serves the most distinguished communities of Dallas, including the Park Cities, Preston Hollow, Bluffview, Devonshire, Uptown, and Turtle Creek — with a legacy resting in consistent, exceptional results across over 100 completed projects.
